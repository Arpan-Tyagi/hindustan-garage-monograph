# Editorial Automotive Photography Prompt Guide
## Hindustan Garage (Est. 1960) & Speed Parts Monograph

This guide provides the complete, field-tested prompt engineering architecture for generating authentic, publication-grade documentary photography for **Hindustan Garage (56, M.G. Marg, George Town)** and **Speed Parts (Medical Chauraha)**.

---

### The "Anti-AI" Prompt Engineering Architecture

Standard AI generation defaults to hyper-saturated, glossy, plastic surfaces, unnatural volumetric lighting, and distorted mechanical geometry. To achieve the aesthetic of high-end automotive monographs (**Singer Vehicle Design**, **Aston Martin Works**, **Leica Camera**, **Patek Philippe**), every prompt follows a strict 6-part anatomical formula:

```
[Subject & Precise Mechanical Action] +
[Authentic Architectural Setting & Realism Details] +
[Camera Body, Specific Optical Focal Length & Aperture] +
[Physical Lighting Geometry & Color Temperature] +
[Film Stock Color Science & Texture Calibration] +
[Aspect Ratio & Anti-CGI Negative Parameters]
```

Shot on Leica SL2, APO-Summicron-SL 50mm f/2 ASPH, f/2.8 aperture, natural clerestory daylight mixed with 3200K warm shop lamps, Kodak Portra 400 color science, authentic workshop textures, subtle natural film grain, zero CGI gloss, zero plastic sheen --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1`
